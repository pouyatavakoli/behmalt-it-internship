## Informal Observations

A few informal observations and notes from the internship that did not fit naturally into the other sections of the documentation:

* KVM switches and dedicated rack monitors were described as relatively expensive equipment and, in some scenarios, considered outdated or unnecessary compared with remote management solutions. This was a practical opinion shared during the internship, not a definitive technical rule.

* The importance of documentation was emphasized repeatedly throughout the internship and was presented as part of the working culture at BehMalt, rather than merely as a desirable technical practice — [Lessons Learned](../lessons-learned/professional/).

## Elecomp Exhibition (Tehran)

Visiting the **Elecomp Exhibition** in Tehran was part of the internship experience. Some of the observations from the visit included:

* Network equipment vendors displayed cable samples with different shielding types, including UTP, FTP, S/FTP, and SF/UTP — [Networking](../it-environment/network/).

* **AI-powered features in task management tools** were visible among several exhibitors.

* A large number of **small startups** were present, alongside vendors showcasing **ERP and CRM** systems. This was relevant to the [Rahkaran ERP](../business-systems/rahkaran-erp/) and [Sarv CRM](../business-systems/sarv-crm/) platforms that were covered later during the internship.

Overall, the exhibition was a useful and enjoyable experience that provided an opportunity to see products and companies from different parts of the industry firsthand. From a personal perspective, however, there were not many particularly notable or unexpected emerging technologies compared with what I had expected.
