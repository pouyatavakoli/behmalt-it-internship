## Standards

The following standards were referenced during networking and infrastructure topics:

* **TIA/EIA-568** — Structured cabling standards and wiring schemes for Ethernet connectors, including **T568A** and **T568B**. See [Passive Networking](../it-environment/network/).

* **IPv4 / IPv6** — The primary Internet addressing standards, covering concepts such as addressing, subnetting, routing, and address allocation.

* **IEEE 802.3 (Ethernet)** — The family of Ethernet standards defining technologies and specifications for local area network communication at the physical and data-link layers, including Fast Ethernet, Gigabit Ethernet, and 10 Gigabit Ethernet.

* **IEEE 802.1Q** — The standard for VLAN tagging in Ethernet networks, used to logically separate networks and carry multiple VLANs over a shared link.

* **ISO/IEC 11801** — An international standard for structured cabling that defines requirements for generic network cabling systems in buildings and other environments.

## Network Certifications

The following certifications were referenced during the internship as relevant networking education and career paths:

* **CCNA (Cisco Certified Network Associate)** — An associate-level certification from Cisco covering networking fundamentals, IP addressing and subnetting, switching and routing, VLANs, basic network security, and network automation.

* **ICND1 / ICND2 (Interconnecting Cisco Networking Devices)** — Cisco's historical exam path for networking fundamentals and CCNA preparation. ICND1 covered basic networking and initial device configuration, while ICND2 built on it with routing, switching, and troubleshooting topics. Cisco retired both exams and the CCENT certification in **February 2020**, replacing the previous path with the single **200-301 CCNA** exam. The terms are now primarily encountered in older study materials and documentation.

* **Network+ (CompTIA)** — A vendor-neutral networking certification covering general networking concepts, infrastructure, network operations, troubleshooting, and basic security.

* **MTCNA (MikroTik Certified Network Associate)** — An entry-level MikroTik certification covering RouterOS fundamentals, including initial device configuration, routing, wireless, firewall, and QoS.

* **MTCRE (MikroTik Certified Routing Engineer)** — A more specialized MikroTik certification focused on routing and advanced routing configuration and protocols in RouterOS.

Together, these certifications cover different areas of networking knowledge: **Network+** focuses on general vendor-neutral concepts, **CCNA** on Cisco environments, and **MTCNA/MTCRE** on MikroTik technologies.
