This section documents the business software systems used at BehMalt alongside the IT infrastructure documented in [IT Environment](../it-environment/overview/).

- [Rahkaran ERP](../business-systems/rahkaran-erp/) — the company's ERP platform, covering core operational processes such as accounting, inventory, and fixed assets.
- [Sarv CRM](../business-systems/sarv-crm/) — the company's CRM platform, used to manage the sales lead pipeline.

Additional business systems (e.g., warehouse/inventory specifics, fixed asset tracking detail, tax, debt collection, entrance/access logging) may be documented here as further internship notes covering hands-on work with those systems become available.
