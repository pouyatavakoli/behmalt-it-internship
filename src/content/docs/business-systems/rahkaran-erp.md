## Overview

BehMalt uses **SystemGroup Rahkaran** as its ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system.

## Concepts

An **ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)** system integrates and manages the core operational processes of an organization within a single connected platform, rather than relying on separate, disconnected tools for each department. A typical ERP centralizes data and workflows across areas such as:

- **Accounting and finance** — general ledger, invoicing, payments
- **Inventory and warehouse management** — stock levels, movements, and locations
- **Fixed assets** — tracking company-owned equipment and property over its lifecycle
- **Procurement** — purchasing and supplier management
- **Production/manufacturing** — relevant for a food-industry company like BehMalt, covering production planning and material usage

Because all of these functions share the same underlying data (e.g., the same product and customer records), an ERP reduces duplicate data entry and keeps information consistent across departments — a change in one module (e.g., a stock movement in the warehouse) is reflected wherever else that data is used (e.g., in accounting or production planning).
