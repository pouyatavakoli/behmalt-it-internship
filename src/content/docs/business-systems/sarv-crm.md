## Overview

BehMalt uses **Sarv** as its CRM (Customer Relationship Management) platform.

## Concepts

A **CRM (Customer Relationship Management)** system manages an organization's interactions with **customers and prospects**, focused on the sales and relationship-building process rather than internal operations. A central concept in CRM is the **lead**: a potential sales opportunity that is created and then tracked through a defined pipeline of stages. Sales staff follow up on a lead — through contact attempts, meetings, proposals, and so on — until it reaches one of two outcomes:

- The lead **converts into an actual customer** (a successful sale), or
- The lead is explicitly **marked as failed/lost**, closing it out of the active pipeline.

This gives an organization visibility into its sales pipeline: how many leads are active, where each one stands in the process, and the conversion rate from lead to customer.

## At BehMalt

**Sarv** is the CRM platform used at BehMalt to manage the sales lead pipeline. Leads are created and tracked through the sales process step by step until they are either converted into customers or marked as failed, giving BehMalt's sales function structured visibility into ongoing sales opportunities and outcomes.
