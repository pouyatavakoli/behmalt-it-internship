This site documents the technical knowledge, systems, and infrastructure encountered during an IT internship at **BehMalt**, The most complete production chain of barley malt and its products in Iran.

Unlike a personal diary, this documentation is organized **by technical subject** rather than by day. Topics covered include passive networking, network infrastructure and security, MikroTik configuration, server room standards, Windows Server administration, and the business systems used at the company.

## Scope

The content reflects what was directly observed, taught, or worked on during the internship. Where notes did not specify exact details (models, configurations, versions), this is marked as `[unclear]` rather than invented.

## Sections summary

- **IT Environment** — the physical and network infrastructure at BehMalt (networking, server room, security, hardware).
- **Business Systems** — company software systems referenced during the internship.
- **Knowledge** — subject-based technical reference material distilled from the internship experience.
- **Procedures & Troubleshooting** — practical workflows and troubleshooting notes.
- **Lessons Learned** — practical and professional takeaways.
- **Internship Log** — a high-level, non-chronological summary of activities and tasks.
