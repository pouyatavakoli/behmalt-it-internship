## Overview

This page covers rack equipment and physical tools used for structured cabling and cable testing at BehMalt.

## Rack Equipment

### Concepts

Server and network racks are measured in **rack units (U)**. One rack unit represents a standard height for mounting equipment. Rack-mount equipment is typically specified by the number of rack units it occupies, such as 2U.

**Blank panels** are used to cover unused rack space and help maintain proper airflow and a clean rack layout.

### At BehMalt

During the internship, the concept of rack units, how equipment occupies rack space, and the purpose of blank panels were reviewed. **Datasheen** was also introduced as a brand associated with rack equipment and panels.

## KVM Switches

A **KVM (Keyboard, Video, Mouse) switch** allows multiple servers to be controlled using a single keyboard, monitor, and mouse.

During the internship, KVM and monitor-based setups were observed. It was also noted that these setups can be costly or unnecessary in some environments, depending on the organization's infrastructure and requirements. This reflects an observation from the internship environment rather than a general recommendation.

## Cable Management and Testing Tools

* **Cable Comb and Organizer** — Used to organize and align groups of cables, particularly in racks and cable pathways.
* **Punch-Down Tool** — Used to terminate and trim cable wires in keystone jacks and patch panels.
* **Wire-Mapping Tester** — Used to check cable continuity, wire mapping, and termination correctness.
* **Fluke Tester** — Professional cable testing and certification equipment used to evaluate link performance against applicable cabling standards.
* **Fiber-Optic Tools** — Used for cleaving and fusion splicing fiber-optic cables. See [Fiber Optics](/it-environment/network/) for more details.

## Power over Ethernet (PoE)

**PoE (Power over Ethernet)** allows both data and electrical power to be transmitted over an Ethernet cable. A PoE-compatible device can receive its power from network equipment such as a PoE switch or injector, eliminating the need for a separate power adapter.

For more details, see [Passive Network and Structured Cabling](/it-environment/network/).
