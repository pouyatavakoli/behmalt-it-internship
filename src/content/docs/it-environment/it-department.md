## Overview

The internship began with an introduction to the IT department as one of the organization's core functions. This included an overview of IT's main responsibilities, its internal structure, and its position within the company's organizational structure.

## Concepts

An IT department typically consists of several main areas, including:

* **Network and Infrastructure** — managing network connectivity, servers, and hardware
* **Security** — protecting systems and data against internal and external threats
* **Systems and Application Support** — supporting business applications such as ERP, accounting, and warehouse systems
* **User Support** — managing access, resolving user issues, and handling helpdesk-related tasks
* **Website and Social Media** — providing technical support for the organization's website and related social media infrastructure

The organizational position of IT affects how it supports business operations. For example, IT may operate as a support function or as a more integrated part of the organization's business processes.

## At BehMalt

The internship introduced the following areas:

* **The main IT functions**, including network and infrastructure, security, servers, and business systems support
* **The organizational position** of the IT department within the company
* How the IT department works on a daily basis to maintain **the stability and continuity of organizational services**, including network management, server administration, and support for business applications such as [Rahkaran Integrated System](/fa/business-systems/rahkaran-erp/)

## Lessons Learned

One of the topics emphasized by the IT staff at BehMalt was the importance of **documentation**. Documenting items such as configurations, MAC addresses, meeting outcomes, and requests helps preserve technical knowledge and makes follow-up and troubleshooting easier.
