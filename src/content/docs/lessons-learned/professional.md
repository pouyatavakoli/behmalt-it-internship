---
title: Professional Lessons
description: Professional/organizational lessons learned during the internship.
---

- **Documentation culture**: BehMalt emphasizes documenting everything — configurations, MAC addresses, and other technical details should be recorded, not just remembered.
- **Meeting and request documentation**: All requests should be documented, and every meeting has a designated person responsible for writing a session report. This ensures accountability and provides a reference for future troubleshooting or audits.
- **Certifications as a career path**: Relevant vendor certifications discussed included MikroTik's MTCNA and MTCRE, and vendor-neutral/other options such as CCNA and Network+, reflecting common paths for growing networking expertise professionally.
