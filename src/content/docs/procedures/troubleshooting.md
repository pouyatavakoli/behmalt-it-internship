The notes did not describe a specific fault/incident troubleshooting scenario in detail. However, the following practices support troubleshooting and were emphasized during the internship:

- Reserving an **empty/unconfigured port on a MikroTik router** for emergency direct access, in case the primary management configuration becomes unreachable — see [MikroTik](/behmalt-it-internship/it-environment/network/#mikrotik).
- Using a **wire-mapping tester** or **Fluke tester** to diagnose cabling issues (opens, shorts, miswires) before assuming a problem is with active equipment — see [Hardware](/behmalt-it-internship/it-environment/hardware/).
- Maintaining **documentation** of configurations and MAC addresses so that issues can be diagnosed and resolved faster by referencing known-good baselines — see [Lessons Learned → Professional](/behmalt-it-internship/lessons-learned/professional/).
